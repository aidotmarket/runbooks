# S1761 Gate 1 — Stripe direct charges for seller sales

**Authority:** Max, 2026-09-29, Event `2c4ff558-3078-481d-8f99-3adf9feb545c`. Design only. Seller is merchant of record for seller sales; its connected account bears processing fees, refunds and disputes. ai.market earns an application fee. No production enablement follows from this spec.

**Evidence baseline:** backend `origin/main` at `e14be3bc0feb644d38806d55707602d88ca93ccb`, read with `git show` on 2026-09-29. All `app/...:line` and `alembic/...:line` references below mean that commit. Local runbook references are to `aidotmarket/runbooks` at this spec's parent commit. Provider behavior: [Stripe direct charges](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges.md?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted), [shared PaymentMethods](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-multiple-accounts), [Connect balances](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/account-balances), [disputes](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/disputes), and [statement descriptors](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/statement-descriptors.md).

## Decisions

1. **Charge and identity.** For every new paid seller order selected for direct mode, create the Checkout Session with `stripe_account=<frozen seller acct>` and `payment_intent_data.application_fee_amount=<platform_fee_cents>`; create an agent PaymentIntent with the same account scope and top-level `application_fee_amount`. Compute once with `split_platform_fee_cents(gross_cents)` and freeze gross, fee, seller, currency, mode, connected account, seller, order, and transaction before provider I/O. Stripe's fee must be positive and less than gross when supplied: for a positive sale whose calculated fee is zero, omit `application_fee_amount` and record zero platform revenue; validate minimum Stripe charge amount separately. No `transfer_data` or later seller Transfer. Use **USD only** for v1: current Checkout line items and serial charges use `usd` (`app/api/v1/endpoints/checkout.py:359-372`; `app/api/v1/endpoints/mcp_marketplace.py:751-763`), while transactions default to USD (`app/models/transaction.py:158-161`). Reject non-USD seller prices before order creation until currency-specific fees, settlement currency, and GL are designed. Let the Standard seller's Stripe account supply the static card statement descriptor and Checkout branding; do not force `AI.MARKET` or a listing title into the descriptor. Show the seller business name and `ai.market` fee in the purchase UI. Stripe uses the connected account descriptor for direct charges ([Stripe descriptors](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/statement-descriptors.md)).
2. **Buyer protection.** Choose **immediate capture plus automatic full refund when delivery definitively fails or its deadline expires**. Preserve buyer confirmation, seven-day auto-confirm, delivery receipts, revocation and dispute controls, but none delays seller access to captured proceeds. A refund request must be idempotent and monitored through the succeeded provider refund event before marking money refunded or terminating the licence. Manual capture would authorize at purchase and capture on buyer/auto confirmation, but normal online card authorization can expire at about seven days; the existing seven-day auto-confirm window starts after delivery (`app/models/order.py:180-181`; `app/services/delivery_completion.py:460`), so slow delivery plus that window cannot reliably fit. An expired authorization would require buyer reapproval and must never grant delivery as paid. Direct capture **loses today's platform-controlled 48-hour post-confirmation transfer hold and the ability to withhold seller funds while delivery/refund questions are resolved** (`app/services/order_money_service.py:834-847,914-923`). Refunds can fail or be delayed after seller payout; Terms and UI must say so.
3. **Seller eligibility.** Require linked Standard account, `charges_enabled=true`, `capabilities.card_payments=active`, `payouts_enabled=true`, and no blocking `requirements.disabled_reason`, past-due/currently-due requirement that disables charges, or deauthorization. Payout readiness alone is insufficient for a direct charge; requiring it as well prevents paid listings from an account unable to receive proceeds. Verify fresh account state at listing publication and immediately before creating a paid checkout/agent PI; refresh on `account.updated`/`capability.updated`, and fail closed on stale or failed provider reads. An unready seller may save a draft and complete Stripe onboarding; its paid listing is unpurchasable across web, MCP, and agent, with a consistent `SELLER_CHARGES_NOT_READY` error. Do not silently fall back to platform charging for a direct-mode order. Existing payout-only gate is at `app/services/seller_setup_service.py:76-88`.
4. **Refunds and disputes.** Buyer requests support through ai.market; seller is the merchant who owes the refund, and Stripe Standard Dashboard may also initiate it. ai.market may submit an authorized policy refund on the connected account, with `stripe_account=<frozen acct>` and `refund_application_fee=true` for full and proportional partial refunds; the platform gives back its commission on refunded value. Record fee refund separately from buyer refund, including asynchronous `application_fee.refunded`. Seller bears unrecovered processing/dispute fees under the verified Stripe fee configuration. For Standard accounts, Stripe first debits the seller balance and can offset future receipts; verify `controller.losses.payments` rather than promise zero platform exposure if a seller balance stays negative ([Stripe balances](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/account-balances), [disputes](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/disputes)). On a dispute, seller responds in Stripe; ai.market records it, revokes buyer access at open, freezes any platform-side discretionary action, and restores/terminates access only after the final outcome and licence rule. Do not route a direct charge through legacy transfer reversal or platform refund admission.
5. **Webhooks.** Configure a **Connect webhook endpoint** for connected-account `checkout.session.completed`, `payment_intent.succeeded`/`payment_failed`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`/`updated`/`closed`, `account.updated`, and `capability.updated`; receive `application_fee.created`/`refunded` on the platform account endpoint and bind them to the connected charge. Retain platform account events for platform-owned services and legacy orders. Verify each endpoint's own signing secret and livemode. For Connect money events require `event.account == order.frozen_account_id`, provider object IDs and amount/currency match the frozen order, and retrieve Session/PI/Charge/Refund with that account scope. Session and PI metadata carry order ID, transaction ID, and payment mode; disputes can map via scoped charge or PI. Keep a durable `(event.id, account, livemode)` idempotency binding, reject contradictions, permit failed-event retry, and commit finance/access effects before 2xx. Account events may have no order; update seller readiness only. The current `normalized_stripe_account` test guard and refund admissions must remain narrow; add separate direct-charge admission (`app/api/v1/endpoints/webhooks.py:277-305,590-685,960-980`; `app/services/refund_processing_service.py:363-429`). Stripe Connect events carry `event.account` ([Stripe Events](https://docs.stripe.com/api/events)).
6. **Cutover and drain.** Add immutable `orders.payment_rail` (`platform_separate` or `seller_direct`), `stripe_account_id`, and a migration/backfill that marks every existing order `platform_separate`. A configured UTC cutover instant **and** per-seller allowlist select direct mode only at order creation; persist the decision atomically. `created_at` is an audit cross-check, never the sole mode detector: retried sessions, delayed webhooks, rollback, and overlapping sellers require the frozen rail. The settlement query, `reserve_order_payout`, `dispatch_order_payout`, `finalize_order_transfer`, confirmation dispatch, and 48-hour hold apply only to `platform_separate`; direct orders have no payout reservation or Transfer and complete commercially when confirmed without a synthetic `settled` Transfer. Keep the hourly settlement job running for legacy orders until their balances, refunds and disputes are reconciled; then disable only its legacy schedule. The two $25 T-891 transactions and the 2026-09-28 order **stay legacy regardless of flag, deployment, or confirmation date**. No recharging, relabeling, or automatic payout; re-audit their live state and follow the separately authorized T-891 recovery path. Rollback changes selection for *new* orders only; existing direct orders continue through their own scoped webhooks/refunds.
7. **Finance and reconciliation.** For direct charges, platform cash and revenue arise only from Stripe's **ApplicationFee** and its fee refund, not gross buyer charge or seller payable. Post `DR Stripe cash (1000) / CR commission revenue (4000)` for actual application fee; reverse proportionally on refund, keyed uniquely by fee/fee-refund ID. Never book the seller's gross cash, seller payable (2110), seller payout (5100), or seller-paid processing fee (6000) in ai.market GL. Keep legacy templates for legacy orders and platform-owned products. Do not reuse billing `payment.net_cents = gross - application_fee_amount` as platform revenue (`app/services/billing_service.py:781-784`). Reconcile order fee expected cents to Stripe `ApplicationFee.amount`, `ApplicationFee.amount_refunded`, platform fee balance transactions, connected charge/Refund/Dispute objects, and the GL by `(account, order, PI, charge, fee)`; handle asynchronously created fees before declaring a row final ([Stripe direct-charge fee reporting](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges.md?platform=web&ui=stripe-hosted)). Daily exceptions need owner and replay path; do not infer Stripe processing fee from the platform fee balance.
8. **Agent saved cards.** Use Stripe's supported per-attempt **PaymentMethod clone** from the platform Customer and saved card to the seller account; the clone is single-use, so do not pass the platform Customer ID into a connected-account PaymentIntent. Freeze the source Customer/PM, connected account, clone outcome, PI request, and idempotency keys in the agent attempt; recover uncertain clone/PI outcomes in seller account scope before retry, and require off-session/SCA eligibility. A declined authentication requirement returns an actionable payment-setup state, not a platform fallback. Stripe supports cloning card and US bank account PMs, but this release is card only ([Stripe sharing guide](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges-multiple-accounts)). Agent purchases ship **last**; until the clone/recovery chunk passes, the direct-mode gate refuses new agent seller purchases (or keeps them on the frozen legacy rail only during an explicitly limited pilot). Full cutover cannot claim completion while agent sales still use platform charges.

## Rollout, gates and tests

- Flag `SELLER_DIRECT_CHARGES_ENABLED`, default false, plus a controlled **per-seller allowlist** and UTC cutover instant. Start with one eligible test seller, then a small production pilot after legal sign-off. Disable the selector to roll back new purchases; retain direct webhook, refund, dispute and reconciliation processing for all existing direct orders. Do not turn off the legacy job while any legacy money remains. Audit fee-payer and negative-balance controller properties for each pilot seller before enabling.
- **Test environment gate:** amend and repin the S1656 money-path environment for a Standard Connect seller and Connect webhook, then run its clean-seed proof with real Stripe test-mode authorization/capture, fee object, seller balance debit, platform fee credit, full and partial refund, dispute, failed delivery, seven-day confirmation semantics, and legacy settlement in parallel. Preserve the environment's lock, exact SHA pins, receipts, and Council/review loop; `money-path-test-environment.md` is the topology/evidence reference and `money-path-proof-loop.md:27-52` defines the repin and `verify --from-clean-seed` loop. A mock webhook, unit suite, or process health result alone is not proof. No production flag before this and signed Terms.
- **Gate 2, reviewable chunks:** (A) immutable rail/account/order binding, cutover selector, migration/backfill and seller readiness contract; (B) charge request and webhook/refund/dispute/finance state-machine design with idempotency and GL mapping; (C) revised Terms and buyer/seller copy, test-environment acceptance contract, and production/rollback gates. Review each chunk against the exact source SHA and reconcile all purchase surfaces.
- **Gate 3, reviewable chunks:** (1) schema, selector and seller eligibility with flag off; (2) web/MCP/canonical Checkout direct charge plus scoped payment webhook; (3) connected refunds, disputes, fee GL and daily reconciliation; (4) legacy/direct settlement branching and drain proof; (5) agent PM clone, off-session recovery and refusal semantics; (6) S1656 repin, real provider proof, Terms deployment, pilot and rollback drill. No chunk enables production by itself.
- **Acceptance tests:** integer fee rounding at $0.19/$0.20/$14.28/$14.29 and partial refunds; USD and zero-price behavior; each charge entry point produces the correct account and no Transfer; unready/revoked seller blocks listing and checkout; connected webhook order binding, duplicate/out-of-order/replayed/forged/wrong-account events; full/partial refund and licence termination, dispute access revoke/restore, negative balance; direct GL equals actual fee/refund balance transactions with no fee expense or payable; legacy T-891 orders still eligible only under old authority; agent clone and unknown-outcome recovery; kill switch changes only new order selection. Browser and agent buyer proof must include actual seller descriptor and terms shown before payment.
