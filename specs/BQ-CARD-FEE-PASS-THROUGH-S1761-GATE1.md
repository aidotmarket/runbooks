## Current path and mismatch

- Paid seller-sale charges are created on the **platform**, without `transfer_data`, by canonical transaction Checkout (`app/services/transaction_service.py:1773-1803`), agent PaymentIntent (`app/services/transaction_service.py:1191-1195`), web Checkout (`app/api/v1/endpoints/checkout.py:353-398`), MCP endpoint (`app/api/v1/endpoints/mcp_marketplace.py:734-774`), and MCP tool (`app/services/mcp_marketplace_tools.py:874-915`). Free orders have no card charge (`app/api/v1/endpoints/checkout.py:317-345`; `app/services/transaction_service.py:1610-1706`). Serial/credit/wallet/data-verification payments and AIM payout are separate products, outside this spec (prior Gate 1 source inventory; no change to their fee rules).
- `PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENTAGE=5.0` and `MINIMUM_TRANSACTION_USD=25.0` are current defaults (`app/core/config.py:696-699`). The fee helper floors commission to cents and sets seller share to the balance (`app/services/fee_calculator.py:8-27`). Today's buyer pays only list price; Stripe debits the platform processing fee, so ai.market's 5% absorbs it. A US 2.9% + 30¢ illustration is not a contractual fee or a safe price floor for international cards or FX.
- The platform captures gross and books seller payable plus commission (`app/services/finance/engine.py:241-256`). The fee expense uses account 6000 (`app/services/finance/engine.py:364-386`; `app/services/finance/gl_service.py:118-132`). The current fee read is opportunistic from an expanded charge on a PaymentIntent (`app/services/finance/payment_service.py:171-184`), insufficient as the payout's authoritative fee snapshot.
- Current seller payout waits 48 hours after the `confirmed` transaction event, or an older order's `escrow_hold_until` (`app/services/order_money_service.py:834-847,914-923`; `app/services/settlement_service.py:46-87`). `reserve_order_payout` freezes a request; `validate_payout_request` insists its amount equals `order.seller_amount_cents`; `dispatch_order_payout` checks provider refunds before `Transfer.create`; finalization journals the transfer (`app/services/order_money_service.py:929-985,1010-1053,1074-1122,1153-1213`). Changing a transfer amount alone would violate those invariants.
- Terms 1.1 instead say buyer pays card/escrow costs above price and card funds are held 90 days by the provider (`app/legal/terms_v1_1.md:19-23,91-95`). Both statements conflict with current card code. The summary and §12.2 say ai.market acts only on court order for disputes (`app/legal/terms_v1_1.md:23,193`), while the platform runs refunds and dispute webhooks (`app/services/refund_processing_service.py:333-429`; `app/api/v1/endpoints/webhooks.py:960-980,1908-1929,1964-1983`).

## Decisions

1. **Keep the money flow.** All seller-sale card charges stay platform-scoped, followed by a separately authorized seller Transfer after delivery, buyer or automatic confirmation, and the existing hold. ai.market remains the party controlling captured platform funds, fee allocation, and transfer timing/amount. This changes neither the seller–buyer licence parties nor the current merchant-of-record position. Do not adopt direct charges, application fees, immediate connected-account proceeds, or new Connect money webhooks. Platform webhook/refund admission remains platform-scoped (`app/services/refund_processing_service.py:363-429`).
2. **One global policy.** Add `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_BEARER={seller,buyer,split}` and, only for `split`, `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_BUYER_SHARE_BPS` (0–10,000); define `seller_share_bps = 10000 - SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_BUYER_SHARE_BPS`. No per-seller or per-listing override in v1. Initial value is `seller`; `buyer` and any split with buyer share above zero are **NEEDS LEGAL SIGN-OFF** and remain disabled until approved. At order creation, atomically freeze policy name, buyer share bps, pricing-estimate version/rate, `P`, `B`, currency, and commission basis. Retries, payment events, refunds, and later global setting changes use that snapshot. Existing orders are explicitly `platform_absorbs`, never inferred from creation date.
3. **Amounts and seller liability.** `order.amount_cents` stays list price `P` in USD cents; the frozen quote record holds buyer Processing fee `B`. The charge/PI/captured-payment guard (`app/services/order_money_service.py:888-893`) must compare `P+B`, while commission and listing-price refunds use `P`. Let `C=floor(0.05P)` (or frozen commission rate), and `A` be the **actual** fee in the charge's platform-scoped `balance_transaction.fee`, with currency and charge identity verified. Policy seller share is `S=A` in seller mode (`B=0`), `S=ceil(A × seller_share_bps/10000)` in split, and `S=0` in buyer mode. Buyer's allocable share is `A-S`. If `B<A-S`, seller also owes gap `G=A-S-B`; otherwise `G=0` and excess `E=B-(A-S)` is refunded to buyer. Final seller recovery is `R=S+G`. When `R<=P-C`, seller transfer `T=P-C-R` and `P=C+R+T` with nonnegative amounts. **The seller bears every Stripe fee; ai.market is never on the hook for any Stripe fee**, including processing fees, Stripe's retained original fee on refund, dispute/chargeback fees and lost dispute amounts. Never substitute a US estimate for `A`. If the fee is absent, unsettled, malformed, wrong-currency, or `R>P-C`, refuse reservation; for the excess record seller receivable `R-(P-C)` and block that seller's payouts until resolved. Do not guess, send a zero/negative Transfer, or silently reduce commission.
4. **Minimum-price floor.** Keep `MINIMUM_TRANSACTION_USD=25.0` (`P>=2500`). At $25, `C=125` and seller share before card fee is $23.75 (`P-C=2375` cents); even an illustrative 10% of $25 plus 30¢ is 280 cents, so the share covers realistic international/FX card fees with substantial room. This illustration is no Stripe guarantee: version a conservative `r_max`/`f_max` ceiling for enabled card geographies/FX and fail closed on the actual `A`. Set `A_max=ceil(r_max(P+B)+f_max)`, `S_max=ceil(A_max × seller_share_bps/10000)` (seller mode `S_max=A_max`, buyer mode `S_max=0`), and `G_max=max(0,A_max-S_max-B)`. The split-mode floor, also used for seller/buyer modes, is `P-C >= S_max+G_max+minimum_transfer` in integer cents, with `B` computed from the ceiling and a positive-transfer minimum where applicable. A real fee above the bound still refuses transfer and creates a seller receivable for any unpaid amount; no platform absorption.
5. **Freeze before dispatch.** Retrieve/expand the charge and its `balance_transaction` in platform scope after capture; verify PI, charge, order, amount `P+B`, currency, livemode and no contradictory refund, then store the fee transaction ID, `A`, `S`, `G`, `R`, `B`, `E` and `T` exactly once under the order-money lock. `order.seller_amount_cents` remains `P-C` to preserve the creation-time equation; the recovered `R` is *discharged*, not still owed. After freeze `remaining_payable_cents=max(P-C-R-seller_refunded_cents-seller_payout_posted_cents,0)`, so before refund/payout it is `T`. `seller_payout_posted_cents` tracks only the actual Transfer `T`, never `R`; refund and transfer journals use the discharged recovery in their independent remaining-payable calculation (`app/services/order_money_service.py:1085-1099`; `app/services/refund_processing_service.py:546`; `app/services/finance/engine.py:268`). `reserve_order_payout` builds the hashed request with `amount=T`, `source_transaction=charge`, destination and idempotency key; `validate_payout_request` independently derives `T` from frozen facts; dispatch rechecks request and provider/refund state before the sole `Transfer.create`. Finalization journals `T` and marks only `T` paid. A retry reuses the frozen fee/request; provider fee mutation is a reconciliation exception, never a rekeyed transfer. `T=0` is a recorded no-transfer terminal outcome; never call Stripe with zero.
6. **Buyer quote.** For buyer mode, show a separate **Processing fee** line before authorization; for split, show the buyer's fraction on that line. Let `b=buyer_share_bps/10000`; use the versioned ceiling `r_max`/`f_max` and freeze `B=ceil[b(r_max P+f_max)/(1-b r_max)]` in integer cents (`0<=r_max<1`, `b r_max<1`). This gross-up accounts for Stripe charging on `P+B`. Captured gross is `P+B`; commission is based on `P` only. Round only once upward to a cent. Any gap `G` is charged to the seller; excess `E` is refunded to the buyer with 4040 reversal, subject to Legal-approved receipt/refund wording. No later debit to the buyer. The line and final total must appear before payment in web, MCP endpoint/tool, canonical transaction Checkout, and agent PaymentIntent consent/quote response; an off-session agent flow without prior exact quote acceptance refuses buyer/split mode. Buyer receipt shows `P`, `B`, fee refund and total.
7. **Refunds, disputes and collection.** Stripe does **not** return the original processing fee on full or partial refund. Freeze `A` once; the original seller fee liability remains and is never credited back on refund. A partial listing-price refund reverses the corresponding commission from `P` and returns the same proportional share of `B` to the buyer, with deterministic final-cent remainder. Refunded `B` becomes additional seller fee liability, up to the full `A` on full refund; reverse its 4040 buyer recovery and recognize matching seller recovery from this order's payable or a receivable. Before transfer, recalculate/refuse net payout under the order lock and re-reserve only before dispatch ownership; any amount beyond remaining proceeds becomes a seller receivable. After transfer, reverse the Transfer where authorized/available, then book any unrecovered refund principal and retained fee as a seller receivable. Apply recovery in order: this order's transfer, then offsets against the **same seller's** later payouts; block all further payouts to that seller while a receivable is open. Record dispute/chargeback fees from the **actual Stripe dispute balance transaction**, never an estimate, and charge that fee and any lost dispute amount to the same seller through the same recovery sequence. The current 5100 refund allocation requires explicit receivable/reversal reconciliation (`app/services/finance/engine.py:258-288`); hold reconciliation until cash, 2110, 4040, 5100 and receivable agree. Full refund sends paid `P+B` to buyer; seller still owes the retained fee. Existing provider-refund admission and payout refusal remain authoritative (`app/services/order_money_service.py:862-897,1175-1189`; `app/services/refund_processing_service.py:333-429`).
8. **Finance and statements.** Preserve actual Stripe processing fee as DR 6000 / CR **1000 Cash - Stripe Clearing**. Gate 3 migration adds **4040 Card fee recovery** to the runtime `MARKETPLACE_CHART` (`app/services/finance/engine.py:157-164`), distinct from 4000 commission. Capture: DR 1000 `P+B`, CR 4000 `C`, CR 2110 `P-C`, CR 4040 `B`. Freeze when payable covers `R`: DR 2110 `R` / CR 4040 `R`; otherwise DR 2110 `P-C` and DR seller receivable `R-(P-C)` / CR 4040 `R`, with no Transfer. Payout when permitted: DR 2110 `T` / CR 1000 `T`. If `B<A-S`, the freeze entry includes seller gap `G`; if `B>A-S`, refund excess `E` with DR 4040 `E` / CR 1000 `E`. Thus 4040 nets to actual `A` in every mode (`B+R-E=A`) before any later buyer-fee refund; that refund reverses buyer recovery and creates equal seller recovery. Journal each event once with distinct source IDs. Seller receivables are assets, remain collectible debts, and offset later seller payouts; 5100 is cleared to receivable where the current refund journal initially uses it. Reconcile charge balance transaction `amount=P+B`, `fee=A`, `net=P+B-A`, currency/ID, fee expense, 4040, refunds, seller payable, Transfer ID/amount and cash. Seller statement shows `P`, commission `C`, fee recovery `R` (including `G`), refunds/receivable, and net `T`.

   **Refund after payout example (seller mode, no Transfer reversal):** `P=2500`, `C=125`, `B=0`, actual `A=S=R=103`, `T=2272`. Capture DR 1000 2500 / CR 2110 2375 / CR 4000 125; Stripe fee DR 6000 103 / CR 1000 103; recovery DR 2110 103 / CR 4040 103; payout DR 2110 2272 / CR 1000 2272. `seller_payout_posted_cents=2272`, discharged recovery is 103, and remaining payable is zero. Full refund DR 4000 125 / DR 5100 2375 / CR 1000 2500; reclassify DR seller receivable 2375 / CR 5100 2375. The receivable is 2272 paid out plus 103 retained Stripe fee. 2110 and 5100 end at zero, 4040 remains 103 against 6000 expense 103; subsequent same-seller payout offsets reduce the receivable until zero.
9. **Legacy and rollout selector.** Add `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_PASS_THROUGH_ENABLED`, default **off**. All orders created before its enablement have immutable `platform_absorbs` and retain today's amounts, fee accounting and payout behavior, including `733ded10-00ca-4619-8a51-716fc2df89c4`, `ed6074fd-617f-45a7-84ea-a20301a026be`, and 2026-09-28 transaction `26f9cf07-87d2-4e09-8590-3d1786d30d66` (dated `specs/T-2026-000891-PAYOUT-PATH-EVIDENCE.md:94-100,145-147`; recheck live state before action). The flag affects **new** orders only. Rollback turns it off for new orders; frozen pass-through orders still use their own fee, refund, journal and payout rules. No automatic relabel, recharge, or payout of legacy orders.
10. **Platform funding and Stripe notice.** Max approved changing the Stripe **platform** payout schedule to **manual** and performs it in the Stripe Dashboard; the Stripe API refuses schedule changes to the platform's own account. API read on 2026-09-29 showed the current schedule as **weekly, Monday, delay 2 days**. T-891 transfer attempts failed for insufficient available platform balance while the platform schedule was automatic weekly payouts, **confirmed from Stripe API responses on 2026-09-29**. `source_transaction` protects pending-charge timing but does not replace the manual schedule for later Transfers ([Stripe separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers)). Verify the Dashboard change, available balance and transfer success in test and production before flag enablement. Stripe Dashboard's direct-charges notice remains advisory for another charge model; do not claim it has been cleared or switch models to dismiss it.

## Terms, law and publication gate

**Max's decision:** keep the code's 48-hour post-confirmation hold. Revised Terms must state that ai.market holds sale proceeds in its Stripe balance, releases them to the seller after delivery confirmation and the hold period, and reserves the right to hold seller proceeds for **up to 90 days**, for example during refund, dispute or fraud review. This 90-day right is contractual; do not build a new 90-day timer. Operator hold uses the existing payout refusal/reconciliation state pending Gate 2 verification of an explicit operator-hold flag. **No pass-through code goes live before revised Terms, checkout copy and acceptance are approved and published.** Preserve the terms version accepted by existing orders.
