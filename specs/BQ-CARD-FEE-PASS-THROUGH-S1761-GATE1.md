## Current path and mismatch

- Paid seller-sale charges are created on the **platform**, without `transfer_data`, by canonical transaction Checkout (`app/services/transaction_service.py:1773-1803`), agent PaymentIntent (`app/services/transaction_service.py:1191-1195`), web Checkout (`app/api/v1/endpoints/checkout.py:353-398`), MCP endpoint (`app/api/v1/endpoints/mcp_marketplace.py:734-774`), and MCP tool (`app/services/mcp_marketplace_tools.py:874-915`). Free orders have no card charge (`app/api/v1/endpoints/checkout.py:317-345`; `app/services/transaction_service.py:1750-1771`). Serial/credit/wallet/data-verification payments and AIM payout are separate products, outside this spec (prior Gate 1 source inventory; no change to their fee rules).
- `PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENTAGE=5.0` and `MINIMUM_TRANSACTION_USD=25.0` are current defaults (`app/core/config.py:696-699`). The fee helper floors commission to cents and sets seller share to the balance (`app/services/fee_calculator.py:8-27`). Today's buyer pays only list price; Stripe debits the platform processing fee, so ai.market's 5% absorbs it. A US 2.9% + 30¢ illustration is not a contractual fee or a safe price floor for international cards or FX.
- The platform captures gross and books seller payable plus commission (`app/services/finance/engine.py:241-256`). The fee expense uses account 6000 (`app/services/finance/engine.py:364-386`; `app/services/finance/gl_service.py:118-132`). The current fee read is opportunistic from an expanded charge on a PaymentIntent (`app/services/finance/payment_service.py:171-184`), insufficient as the payout's authoritative fee snapshot.
- Current seller payout waits 48 hours after the `confirmed` transaction event, or an older order's `escrow_hold_until` (`app/services/order_money_service.py:834-847,914-923`; `app/services/settlement_service.py:46-87`). `reserve_order_payout` freezes a request; `validate_payout_request` insists its amount equals `order.seller_amount_cents`; `dispatch_order_payout` checks provider refunds before `Transfer.create`; finalization journals the transfer (`app/services/order_money_service.py:929-985,1010-1053,1074-1122,1153-1213`). Changing a transfer amount alone would violate those invariants.
- Terms 1.1 instead say buyer pays card/escrow costs above price and card funds are held 90 days by the provider (`app/legal/terms_v1_1.md:19-23,91-95`). Both statements conflict with current card code. Terms also say payment disputes involve only buyer and seller and ai.market acts only on court order (`app/legal/terms_v1_1.md:191-195`), while the platform runs refunds and dispute webhooks (`app/services/refund_processing_service.py:333-429`; `app/api/v1/endpoints/webhooks.py:960-980,1908-1929,1964-1983`).

## Decisions

1. **Keep the money flow.** All seller-sale card charges stay platform-scoped, followed by a separately authorized seller Transfer after delivery, buyer or automatic confirmation, and the existing hold. ai.market remains the party controlling captured platform funds, fee allocation, and transfer timing/amount. This changes neither the seller–buyer licence parties nor the current merchant-of-record position. Do not adopt direct charges, application fees, immediate connected-account proceeds, or new Connect money webhooks. Platform webhook/refund admission remains platform-scoped (`app/services/refund_processing_service.py:363-429`).
2. **One global policy.** Add `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_BEARER={seller,buyer,split}` and, only for `split`, `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_BUYER_SHARE_BPS` (0–10,000; seller share is the remainder). No per-seller or per-listing override in v1: one published rule is easier to explain, reconcile, and audit. Recommended initial value is `seller`; `buyer` and any split with buyer share above zero are **NEEDS LEGAL SIGN-OFF**. At order creation, atomically freeze policy name, buyer share bps, pricing-estimate version/rate, list price, buyer fee quote, currency, and platform commission basis. Retries, payment events, refunds, and a later global setting change use that snapshot. Existing orders are explicitly `platform_absorbs`, never inferred from creation date.
3. **Amounts and seller mode.** Let `P` be list price in USD cents, `C=floor(0.05P)` (or the frozen commission rate), `B` the separately displayed buyer processing-fee quote, and `A` the **actual** fee in the charge's platform-scoped `balance_transaction.fee`, with transaction currency and charge identity verified. For seller mode `B=0`, seller recovery `S=A`, seller transfer `T=P-C-S`; require `P=C+S+T` and all three nonnegative. For split, `S=ceil(A × seller_share_bps/10,000)`, buyer's actual allocable share is `A-S`, and `T=P-C-S`. For buyer mode `S=0`, `T=P-C`. Never substitute a US estimate for `A`: international-card and FX fees can be higher. If Stripe's fee is absent, unsettled, malformed, mismatched in currency, or cannot fit in the seller share, refuse reservation and raise a reconciliation case; do not guess, make a negative Transfer, reduce commission silently, or dispatch from stale facts.
4. **Minimum-price floor.** Before a paid checkout, require `P-C` to cover a published conservative card-fee ceiling for seller mode or the seller fraction in split mode, plus Stripe's minimum positive transfer where applicable. For an estimate `r_max` of proportional fees and `f_max` fixed cents on the charge, the seller-mode bound is `P-floor(cP) >= ceil(r_max P+f_max)+minimum_transfer`; solve it in integer cents, with buyer gross-up included in the charge for split. The configured ceiling must cover enabled card geographies/FX and be versioned; the current $25 minimum is not proof. If a real fee still exceeds the share, freeze a no-transfer exception for Max/finance to resolve under the refund or separately authorized seller-receivable policy. No automatic platform subsidy or negative seller balance.
5. **Freeze before dispatch.** Retrieve/expand the charge and its `balance_transaction` in platform scope after capture; verify PI, charge, order, amount, currency, livemode and no contradictory refund, then store the fee transaction ID, actual fee, seller recovery `S`, buyer quote `B`, and net transfer `T` exactly once under the order-money lock. `reserve_order_payout` builds the existing hashed request with `amount=T`, `source_transaction=charge`, destination and idempotency key; `validate_payout_request` independently derives `T` from the immutable order policy and frozen fee record rather than `order.seller_amount_cents`; `dispatch_order_payout` rechecks that same request and provider/refund state before the sole `Transfer.create`. Finalization journals `T` and marks only `T` paid. A retry reuses the frozen fee and request; any provider fee mutation becomes a reconciliation exception, never a rekeyed transfer. Preserve the current no-transfer or explicit zero-net handling without calling Stripe with zero.
6. **Buyer quote.** For buyer mode, show a separate **Processing fee** line before authorization; for split, show the buyer's fraction on the same line. Let `b=buyer_share_bps/10,000`, `r` be the versioned estimated proportional Stripe rate, and `f` the fixed charge fee in cents. Freeze `B=ceil[b(rP+f)/(1-br)]` in integer cents (`0<=r<1`, `br<1`); this gross-up accounts for Stripe charging on `P+B`. The captured charge is `P+B`; commission remains based on `P`, never `B`. Round only once upward to a cent. The platform covers any shortfall between `B` and the buyer's actual allocable share; if `B` exceeds it, refund the excess to the buyer and reverse that recovery, subject to legal-approved receipt/refund wording. No later debit to the buyer. The line and final total must appear before payment in web, MCP endpoint, MCP tool, canonical transaction Checkout, and agent PaymentIntent consent/quote response; an off-session agent flow without prior exact quote acceptance must refuse buyer/split mode. Buyer receipt shows list price, Processing fee, any fee adjustment/refund, and total.
7. **Refund allocation.** Stripe does **not** return the original processing fee on full or partial refund. Do not return the seller's original card-fee charge as a payout credit. Freeze `A` once and do not recalculate it from the post-refund net. A partial listing-price refund reduces remaining seller proceeds and reverses the corresponding commission under the existing refund allocation; return the same proportional share of `B` to the buyer, with deterministic cent remainder on the final refund. The original `S` remains seller responsibility. Before transfer, recalculate/refuse the net payout under the order lock and re-reserve a new request only if no dispatch ownership exists; if remaining proceeds cannot cover `S`, record the shortfall as a seller receivable for authorized recovery, not negative payout. After transfer, implement an authorized Transfer reversal where available or record a seller receivable for unrecovered amounts; the current refund journal's 5100 allocation needs an explicit reconciliation design, not an assumed existing reversal (`app/services/finance/engine.py:258-288`). Mark reconciliation required until cash and GL agree. A full refund sends the full paid amount `P+B` back to the buyer and leaves Stripe's original fee as expense offset by collectible seller recovery only. Legal must approve this retained-fee/receivable term before enablement. Existing provider-refund admission and payout refusal stay authoritative (`app/services/order_money_service.py:862-897,1175-1189`; `app/services/refund_processing_service.py:333-429`).
8. **Finance and statements.** Preserve actual Stripe processing fee as DR 6000 / CR Stripe cash. Add account **4040, Card fee recovery** (revenue, distinct from 4000 commission). At capture, DR Stripe cash `P+B`, CR commission 4000 `C`, CR seller payable 2110 `P-C`, and CR buyer fee recovery 4040 `B`. When `A` is frozen, DR seller payable 2110 `S` / CR 4040 `S`; transfer then DR remaining payable `T` / CR Stripe cash `T`. Journal each event once with distinct source IDs; refund/receivable journals preserve balance. Add 4040 to P&L/reporting; never misstate it as commission or net it invisibly into 6000. Reconcile charge balance transaction `amount=P+B`, `fee=A`, `net=P+B-A`, fee transaction ID and currency to fee expense, recovery, refunds, seller payable, Transfer ID/amount and platform residual. Seller dashboard and payout statement show `P`, ai.market fee `C`, card processing fee `S`, refunds/receivable, and net `T`; seller-bears examples must never promise an unconditional 95% net.
9. **Legacy and rollout selector.** Add `SELLER_SALE_CARD_FEE_PASS_THROUGH_ENABLED`, default **off**. All orders created before its enablement have immutable `platform_absorbs` and retain today's amounts, fee accounting and payout behavior, including `733ded10-00ca-4619-8a51-716fc2df89c4`, `ed6074fd-617f-45a7-84ea-a20301a026be`, and 2026-09-28 transaction `26f9cf07-87d2-4e09-8590-3d1786d30d66` (dated `specs/T-2026-000891-PAYOUT-PATH-EVIDENCE.md:94-100,145-147`; recheck live state before action). The flag affects **new** orders only. Rollback turns it off for new orders; frozen pass-through orders still use their own fee, refund, journal and payout rules. No automatic relabel, recharge, or payout of legacy orders.
10. **Platform funding and Stripe notice.** Max owns the Stripe **platform** payout schedule: set it to manual or delayed enough to retain charge proceeds through confirmation, 48-hour hold and transfer retries. Automatic platform bank payouts can drain available funds before the post-confirmation Transfer, yielding insufficient funds as seen in T-2026-000891. `source_transaction` protects pending-charge timing but does not replace a platform funding schedule for this later transfer ([Stripe separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers)). Check schedule, available balance and transfer success in test and production before flag enablement. Stripe Dashboard's direct-charges recommendation/notice remains because seller Standard accounts exist; it is advisory for another charge model. Our chosen platform charges, controlled hold and separate Transfers make that notice acceptable; do not claim it has been cleared or switch models to dismiss it.
