## BETTER

**Advisory only:** a first-class `agent_payment_attempts` table keyed by immutable `attempt_id` and unique `transaction_id`, with explicit revision/state/spend-release columns and immutable request columns, would be materially stronger than JSONB metadata.

- **Failure-mode comparison:** It would make whole-object `transactions.metadata` replacement unable to erase the attempt authority and would provide a direct database uniqueness/ownership anchor. The current design depends on service discipline and the metadata-writer inventory in NIT 1.
- **Cost:** One additional migration/table and query/join path; the 28-field record would still require exact validation. It would also expand the currently selected 27-target implementation scope.
- **Rollback:** Like the selected forward-only design, retirement would need separately reviewed migration work. A dedicated table is harder to retire than metadata but easier to audit and constrain.
- **Disposition:** Advisory only. The JSONB design can preserve the invariants if NIT 1’s final writer inventory is added; no demonstrated current failure mandates the schema expansion.
